About the Show

Stories from the Shadows

SL Tonight is a Second Life storytelling channel inspired by The Twilight Zone, Tales from the Crypt, and the grown-up version of Reading Rainbow nobody made until now.


Your host Felix Lubezki presents horror readings, original music from The Dial Tones, retro comic readings, nature documentaries about digital cats, paranormal file reviews, and whatever else the night brings. Filmed in Second Life. Presented everywhere else.


"We are where the nightmares go."

What's On

The Programming

Show 01
Tales That Terrify
Readings from Alfred Hitchcock's anthology collections. Horror stories presented with the care they deserve. Series 2 now airing.
Show 02
On Record: Believe It Or Don't
Documented anomalies from Charles Fort's Book of the Damned. Things that fell from clear skies. The explanations. The geometry of it. It is on record.
Show 03
The Dial Tones
Original music inspired by retro games, arcade culture, and the things nobody made songs about yet. The house band of SL Tonight.
Show 04
SL Toons
Public domain cartoon readings, nature documentaries about digital cats, and the Midnight Series — classic animation scored with Dial Tones originals.
Show 05
Atari Comics
Full readings of the Atari Force comic series. Felix reads them out loud in Second Life. Somehow that's a thing that exists now.
Show 06
Borrowed Worlds
Fan fiction readings presented with care. Currently: Star Fox: Shards of the Past by InsurrectionAlpha. These are borrowed worlds. They deserved to be heard.
Show 07
Witches Tales & Friends
Full issue readings of public domain horror comics. Harvey Comics, EC Comics, and more. Ten cents worth of weird yarns of unseen terrors.
