About the Game

Knockout 2: Wrath of the Karen

As an FBI agent, research a terrifying disease spreading across the country in this classic Punch-Out!! inspired game. A much darker, older secret lurks in the shadows — an ancient evil rises to threaten national security, the world, and your quarterly performance bonus.


Now live with 8 fights and a full Prestige system, Missions, Equipment, and more. iOS release pending review.

What's Inside

Features

Everything packed into the global launch of Knockout 2.

🥊
8 Fights + Prestige
Complete the base 8 fights, then push further with the all-new Prestige system. One prestige level live now, with a second currently in the works.
📋
Missions System
Daily, weekly, and monthly missions — all procedurally generated so every player gets the same missions (except the Personal Daily, unique to you). Earn Fed Creds and compete with friends to see who clears them fastest.
🧰
Equipment System
Stat-modifying gear that changes how you fight or gives you an edge in the ring. More equipment coming soon.
🎨
Customization
A new, more advanced character customization system — currently featuring clothing colors with more options, including a male character, targeted for an upcoming release.
🎬
Cutscenes & Story
Knockout cutscenes for all characters, an updated pre-fight story dialogue system with an actual storyline, an enhanced News Scene, and unlockable lore articles on the Match Select screen.
🌐
Localization & Controls
Community translations for Portuguese, Spanish, and French. Full controller and keyboard remapping in-game, with more languages in the works.
